## Pricing: What You Actually Pay in 2026

### Housecall Pro

Housecall Pro publishes its pricing, but the number you see on the homepage is not always what HVAC contractors end up paying. The platform runs a trade-specific pricing quiz that surfaces higher rates for HVAC and plumbing than the base page shows.

Published annual billing rates as of Q3 2026:

- **Basic:** $59/month — 1 user, scheduling and invoicing only, no QuickBooks sync, limited support
- **Essentials:** $149/month — up to 5 users, two-way QuickBooks, marketing automation, membership billing, online booking
- **MAX:** $299/month — up to 8 users, dedicated onboarding, advanced reporting, $35/user overage

For a 5-tech HVAC shop on the Essentials plan, the real monthly cost typically lands between $300 and $400 once payment processing fees are factored in. Housecall Pro charges a processing fee on every card transaction, which adds up quickly for shops running $15,000 to $30,000 per month in card volume.

The 14-day free trial gives full access to the MAX plan, which is genuinely useful for evaluating features before committing.

### Jobber

Jobber also publishes pricing. As of Q3 2026, annual billing plans are:

- **Core:** $49/month — 1 user, basic scheduling and invoicing, no route optimization
- **Connect:** $149/month — up to 5 users, QuickBooks two-way sync (one source says one-way on Core), online booking, automated follow-ups
- **Grow:** $249/month — up to 15 users, route optimization, marketing tools, referrals, automated quote follow-ups
- **Plus:** $599/month — teams up to 15 with advanced features; custom above that

Payment processing on Jobber runs 2.9% + $0.30 per card transaction and 1% for ACH bank payments. For HVAC shops with high average ticket sizes — $400 to $1,200 per service call — the processing fees on Jobber and Housecall Pro are roughly equivalent.

**Bottom line on pricing:** At the mid-tier where most 3 to 8-tech HVAC shops land, Housecall Pro Essentials and Jobber Connect are both $149/month with similar user limits. The decision is not about price — it is about which platform's features at that price point match your operation.

## Housecall Pro for HVAC: Where It Wins

### Maintenance agreements are its strongest feature

For HVAC companies that run seasonal tune-up programs or annual maintenance contracts, Housecall Pro's membership billing is the most consistently praised feature in HVAC-specific reviews. The platform automates renewal billing, generates scheduled maintenance visits, and lets customers renew online without calling the office.

Multiple HVAC owners on G2 and Capterra specifically cite this as the reason they chose Housecall Pro over Jobber. One reviewer running a residential HVAC shop noted making the switch from Jobber specifically for the sales proposal tools and maintenance agreement automation. Recurring service revenue is what keeps HVAC margins healthy in off-peak months — and Housecall Pro builds a real system around it.

### Marketing automation at a lower plan tier

Housecall Pro includes automated post-job review requests, email campaigns, and customer follow-up tools starting at the Essentials plan ($149/month). Jobber puts comparable marketing features behind the Grow plan ($249/month), which is $100/month more for the same number of users.

For an HVAC shop that wants to build Google reviews consistently and run seasonal tune-up campaigns without hiring a marketing coordinator, Housecall Pro delivers those tools $100/month cheaper than Jobber does.

### The dispatch board for residential HVAC teams

Housecall Pro's visual dispatch calendar is specifically well-regarded for residential HVAC shops running 5 to 15 techs across multiple service zones. The drag-and-drop board gives dispatchers a clean view of capacity, zone coverage, and job status in real time. Contractors on r/HVAC consistently describe it as the most intuitive dispatch experience at this price point.

### iOS users get the better app

If your team runs iPhones, Housecall Pro's iOS app rates at 4.6 — matching Jobber on iOS. But on Android, the gap is significant: Jobber's Android app rates 4.7, while Housecall Pro's sits at 3.3. If any of your techs use Android devices, that gap matters operationally. If your entire team is on iOS, both apps perform comparably.

### Who Housecall Pro is right for

Housecall Pro is the stronger pick for HVAC contractors who:

- Run 2 to 15 technicians on primarily residential service work
- Have an active or planned maintenance agreement program
- Want marketing automation without paying Grow-tier prices
- Run a team entirely on iOS devices
- Need fast deployment with minimal training

## Jobber for HVAC: Where It Wins

### Route optimization is a real operational advantage

This is the most significant functional difference between the two platforms in 2026. Jobber added automatic route optimization in 2025 and it is now included on the Grow plan and above. Housecall Pro does not offer route optimization at any price point as of Q3 2026.

For HVAC shops running 4 or more trucks with 8 to 15 service calls per truck per day, the savings are measurable. One plumbing contractor on r/sweatystartup documented switching from Housecall Pro to Jobber after 14 months specifically because the lack of routing was costing his 4 trucks roughly 45 minutes of avoidable drive time per day, per truck. At loaded labor rates, that is real money lost every operating day.

For HVAC shops with high daily stop counts — particularly in suburban or rural markets where drive time compounds quickly — Jobber's route optimization alone can justify the Grow plan upgrade over Housecall Pro's MAX tier.

### Cleaner quoting and estimate workflow

Jobber's quoting interface is consistently rated as the cleaner of the two for estimate-heavy HVAC work. For shops that quote system replacements, ductwork modifications, or commercial installs — where proposals need to look professional and follow-up matters — Jobber's automated quote follow-up reminders and client hub approval flow are noticeably better than Housecall Pro's equivalent.

One verified Capterra reviewer running an HVAC/R filtration business specifically cited better customization and automation as the reason they chose Jobber over Housecall Pro after evaluating both.

### Wisetack consumer financing on all plans

High-ticket HVAC work — furnace replacements, full AC system installs, heat pump conversions — is where consumer financing directly impacts close rates. Jobber integrates with Wisetack for customer financing on all plan tiers. Housecall Pro offers financing through integrations as well, but the availability and plan requirements differ.

For HVAC contractors whose average install ticket exceeds $3,000, a financing option presented at point of estimate closes jobs that would otherwise require the customer to "think about it." Jobber's broader Wisetack availability is a genuine advantage for replacement-heavy shops.

### Jobber Copilot: AI tools Housecall Pro doesn't have

Jobber launched Copilot as an AI assistant integrated into the platform. As of 2026, Housecall Pro has no equivalent feature. Copilot handles tasks like drafting customer responses, summarizing job history, and surfacing recommended actions. It is not a transformative feature for every shop, but for owner-operators managing the office solo, it reduces administrative time on routine communication.

### Android team compatibility

If your technicians use Android devices — common across HVAC crews that have been issued company phones over multiple years — Jobber's Android app rates significantly higher than Housecall Pro's. A field team that resists software because the app crashes or performs poorly on their phones is an adoption problem that costs more than the software subscription.

### Who Jobber is right for

Jobber is the stronger pick for HVAC contractors who:

- Run 4 or more trucks with route-heavy daily scheduling
- Do significant estimate or proposal work for replacements and installs
- Have Android users on their field team
- Want AI-assisted office tools
- Prioritize high-ticket financing options at the point of estimate

## The Verdict: Which One Wins for HVAC?

**Choose Housecall Pro if your revenue model is built around maintenance agreements and recurring residential service.** The membership billing automation, the marketing tools at the Essentials tier, and the polished iOS app make it the most efficient platform for HVAC shops that monetize through seasonal tune-up programs and annual service contracts. If you run 2 to 10 techs doing primarily residential maintenance and repair, Housecall Pro is the better fit at the $149/month price point.

**Choose Jobber if your HVAC operation is route-heavy or replacement-focused.** The route optimization alone separates Jobber from Housecall Pro for shops running 4 or more trucks across spread-out service areas. Add in the cleaner quoting workflow, Wisetack financing availability, and better Android app performance, and Jobber wins for HVAC businesses where installation and estimate volume drives revenue alongside service calls.

**The one scenario where neither is the right answer:** If you are running 10 or more trucks, generating over $3M in annual revenue, and managing a combination of residential and commercial accounts, both platforms will start showing their ceilings. At that scale, the ServiceTitan conversation becomes relevant — with all the cost and implementation complexity that comes with it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Housecall Pro or Jobber better for a small HVAC company?**  
For an HVAC company with 1 to 5 technicians, both platforms are viable at similar price points. Housecall Pro has an edge if maintenance agreements are central to your business model. Jobber has an edge if you do significant quoting work or run route-heavy schedules.

**Does Housecall Pro have route optimization?**  
No. As of Q3 2026, Housecall Pro does not offer route optimization at any plan tier. Jobber added route optimization in 2025 and includes it on the Grow plan ($249/month annual) and above.

**Can I switch from Housecall Pro to Jobber without losing my data?**  
Both platforms support data export, and Jobber's onboarding team assists with migration for new accounts on the Connect plan and above. The process typically takes 1 to 2 weeks of parallel running before fully switching over.

**Which platform is better for HVAC maintenance agreements?**  
Housecall Pro. Its membership billing feature automates recurring charges, visit scheduling, and online renewal for maintenance agreement customers. Multiple HVAC contractors specifically cite this as the reason they chose Housecall Pro over Jobber.

**Does Jobber work for commercial HVAC?**  
Jobber handles commercial accounts at a functional level — scheduling, quoting, and invoicing work for commercial clients. For contractors with significant commercial maintenance contract volume across multiple facilities, ServiceTitan's commercial capabilities are more robust. Jobber is strongest for residential-primary HVAC businesses with some light commercial work mixed in.
