## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the rigor of investigative enterprise journalism fused with the industrial restraint of high-end consumer technology interfaces. Designed for enterprise executives, procurement leaders, and software architects, the experience prioritizes institutional authority, forensic clarity, and contemplative readability over flashiness.

### Design Movement
**Architectural Minimalism & High-End Editorial:**
- Grounded in expansive negative space, structured grid alignments, and hairline separation rules.
- Typography drives emotional resonance: literary serifs project academic integrity and investigative depth, balanced with ultra-clean, technical sans-serifs for navigation, quantitative benchmarks, and comparative scorecards.
- Visual elements exhibit zero decorative bloat; meaning is conveyed through weight, spatial proportion, and pristine hierarchy.

## Colors

The system uses a calibrated palette reflecting classic editorial ink on archival stock, updated with modern digital precision.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#0B132B` / Deep Midnight Navy):** The definitive tone for lead headers, authoritative branding, masthead containers, and key interactive focal points.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB` / Electric Royal Blue):** Reserved for hyperlinks, primary action prompts, verified badges, and interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` / Editorial Amber Gold):** Dedicated exclusively to evaluation metrics: review stars, benchmark indicators, score highlights, and tier rankings.
- **Neutral Primary (`#090D16` / Deep Obsidian):** Used for standard running text and high-contrast borders in inverted mode.

### Tonal Hierarchy & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Crisp White) for primary editorial articles; `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) for secondary backdrops, sidebar panels, and data card fills.
- **Hairline Dividers:** `#E2E8F0` and `#E5E7EB` create razor-thin 1px grid structures, preventing visual visual weight while maintaining separation.
- **Subdued & Caption Ink:** `#64748B` (Slate 500) ensures accessible, understated metadata, bylines, and timestamps without competing with headlines.

## Typography

The typographic architecture balances humanistic gravitas with computational precision.

- **Newsreader (Editorial Serif):** Employs soft optical variations tailored for long-form reading, executive investigations, and lead editorial headlines. It gives critical software reviews the weight of print investigative publications.
- **Inter (Functional Body):** Delivers clean optical tracking for UI copy, dense software feature summaries, and multi-paragraph technical teardowns.
- **Geist (Technical Metadata & Numbers):** Imparts a monoline, technical feel for ratings, dates, author bylines, data matrices, and enterprise pricing grids.

### Implementation Rules
- Titles use standard headline capitalization without forced uppercase, except for `label-caps` which must always be uppercase.
- Long-form article text is bounded to a maximum width of 680px to preserve an optimal 65–75 character measure.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column architectural grid inspired by modernist editorial layouts. Whitespace functions as a primary structural element, separating complex software evaluations into digestible sections.

### Breakpoints & Responsive Behavior
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px lateral padding. Complex comparison tables switch to horizontal swipe cards or vertical metric lists.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, 24px lateral padding. Dual-column layouts for review summaries and feature breakdowns.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, 32px gutters, centered within `1280px` maximum width. Supports an asymmetrical 8-column reading stream paired with a 4-column persistent scorecard / index sidebar.

### Vertical Rhythm
Section blocks adhere to an 8px base rhythm, utilizing `space-3xl` (64px) to `space-4xl` (96px) between major investigative chapters to create clear visual separation.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids skeuomorphic dropshadows and heavy blurs in favor of flat planar stratification and hairline borders.

### The Planar Hierarchy
1. **Canvas (Level 0):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` editorial body canvas.
2. **Substrate (Level 1):** `#F8FAFC` surface used for technical sidebars, rating badges, key quote callouts, and data cards, bounded by 1px `#E2E8F0` borders.
3. **Floating Navigation & Overlays (Level 2):** Frosted glass effect (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with a clean bottom hairline of `#E5E7EB`.
4. **Modals & Flyouts (Level 3):** Grounded by a subtle, ambient diffusion: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.07)`, paired with a strict 1px border.

## Shapes

The visual geometry balances architectural precision with quiet elegance. Sharp, structured edges predominate, softened only slightly on interactive touchpoints.

- **Base Radius (`4px` / `0.25rem`):** Applied to tags, filter chips, rating badges, and action buttons. Keeps elements looking crisp and professional without feeling harsh.
- **Card & Container Radius (`6px` to `8px`):** Applied to data tables, scorecards, and media containers.
- **Circular (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for author avatars, operational status pips, and isolated iconography.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#0B132B` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` border radius, high-density padding (`10px 18px`). Text set in `label-data`. On hover, transitions cleanly to `#1F2937`.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent fill with a 1px `#E2E8F0` hairline border, `#0B132B` text. On hover, shifts to `#F8FAFC` with border color `#0B132B`.
- **Editorial Text Action:** Unbordered, blue (`#2563EB`) text with an underline offset of `4px` that transitions to dark obsidian on hover.

### Software Scorecard & Rating Chips
- **Overall Score Indicator:** Bold, monoline Geist typography set within a `#0B132B` dark container or framed by a hairline border, accompanied by `#F59E0B` rating stars.
- **Taxonomy Chips / Category Tags:** Tiny, high-density labels set in `label-caps`. Background `#F1F5F9`, foreground `#0B132B`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`. Hover state switches to inverse colorway.

### Lists & Comparison Matrix
- **Review Pros & Cons:** Clean horizontal blocks. Pros lead with a minimal emerald marker, cons with an understated slate-gray dash. No cartoonish icons.
- **Data Tables:** Strict 1px hairline horizontal rules (`#E5E7EB`). Headers in `label-caps` with `#64748B` ink. Alternate row zebra striping is omitted in favor of hover highlighting with `#F8FAFC`.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Search Bar:** Minimalist full-width or inline search input. Clean 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `4px` radius, typography set in `body-md`. Focus state uses a crisp `#0B132B` 1px border without aggressive outer glow rings.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Scaled down to `16px`. Deep midnight `#0B132B` checked fill with a pure white glyph.

### Editorial & Review Cards
- **Article Teaser:** Clean vertical stack consisting of category label (`label-caps`), Newsreader headline (`headline-lg`), summary excerpt (`body-md`), and Geist metadata row. Separated by subtle bottom borders rather than boxed card frames.
- **Software Benchmark Card:** Shallow `#F8FAFC` backdrop with a hairline border. Contains a structured grid: product title, verified verdict score, price tier, and quick-link CTA.
